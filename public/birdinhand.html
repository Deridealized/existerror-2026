Our Story

A Pub That's Stood Since the 15th Century

Nestled beside a 14th century church in the quiet village of Austrey, the Bird in Hand has been welcoming travellers, locals, and wanderers for over six hundred years. Original beams, stone floors, and gas fires in both the bar and lounge — some things, thankfully, don't change.

Gareth has been your landlord since 2009. Samuel, a 46-year-old village native, has run the bar since 2010. Paul has kept the kitchen honest for just as long. And Ralph, our resident pub dog, ensures everyone feels at home the moment they walk through the door.
